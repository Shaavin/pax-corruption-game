## Resolved (2026-09-01)

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Deck math / pile E | Starting hands (3+4) come out **before** the market. Then 5 non-GE market cards. Remaining **92** cards match the printed A–E sizes exactly. E is **not** “the rest.” |
| Chaos piles | A `12+1`, B `10`, C+D one pile `60&3`, E `10`. GE: 1 under A, 3 shuffled into the 60. |
| General election art | Placeholder faces until official art exists. |
| Starting hands | First player takes **3 of 4** listed cards. Second player takes **all 4**. Then shuffle remaining non-GE cards and deal the market of 5. |
| National Priorities leftovers | The 2 never-revealed monuments go into the **monument deck**. Boxed choices stay out. |
| Legal Review | Targeted district becomes **Neutral**. District Governor does **not** block this (it only names policy referendum). |
| Election-phase income | After taking a market card, you may still draw from the **deck** if under hand limit. Market is not replenished. |
| Referendum district order | Left → right as laid out. “First player” = **setup** first player. |
| Policy change options | That district’s **other printed side**, or **Neutral**. Neutral may be active in multiple districts. |
| Smart Doctrine symbols | Owner scores **one of each** type. |
| Planet Next | Before election results, owner may discard cards **from hand**. Each discarded card gives **+2 influence this election only** to its **printed district**. Cards go to support piles (usual 1 influence after that) and can immediately trip Popularity. |
| Space Agency | Whenever **you** discard, you may send cards to **your** other district piles, at most 1 per other district. Never moves opponent discards. Does not change Martial Law spend checks (printed district of cards paid). United Front applies only if the chosen landing pile is Smarbbit (then the opponent’s Smarbbit pile gets it). A Smarbbit-printed card routed elsewhere stays yours. |
| Council of Lords winner | Count **printed handshake symbols**. Electors for Lords remain “played alliance cards” unless later corrected. |
| Executive power at start | Nobody holds it until the first election is won. |
| Digital seating | District order locked at setup; always left-to-right as displayed. |
| Politics step pairing | Executive power and political campaign may **both** be used on the same turn. Campaign tuck is one card from hand, **including conspiracy**. Alliance = 2 policy support; other tucked cards (civil or conspiracy) = 1 in their printed district. |
| Martial Law | Limits apply **only in Shavvinne**. −1 alliance limit there (default 1 → 0; Model Society 2 → 1). Recruit / Construct / Referendum stay legal if they do not discard 2+ Shavvinne cards. A Shavvinne conspiracy wipes many cards there, so it is illegal under Martial Law. Other districts unaffected. |
| Consultative Office | Each user gets a **personal face-up zone** beside them. Move **Smarbbit cards from hand** into it. Usable later **as if in hand**; they do **not** count toward hand limit. |
| Currency Ballots | A general election drawn by this effect does **not** delay the mandatory discard. Finish draw-then-discard, then apply normal GE timing (set aside / replace / election after the current resolution). |
| United Front | If a card is discarded **to Smarbbit** (the landing pile), it goes to the **non-discarding** player's Smarbbit pile. If Space Agency (or similar) sends it somewhere else, the discarder keeps it. All discard sources. Can feed Popularity. |
| Dragon Gate | Extra draw is its **own step after normal income**. Check hand size then. A GE drawn here does **not** interrupt this step. |
